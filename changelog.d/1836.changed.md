- **Code generation reads only the obligation table's row for a refinement guard at a construction store or a handler clause binder** ([#1836](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1836)). The roster of guarded positions (`narrowing.REFINED_BIND_GUARDED_SITES`) and the two gates that read it beside the row are gone; every position it named was a member at every reader, so no check moves.
- **`vera verify` discharges every obligation from the obligation table's rows** ([#1836](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1836), Stage 27). The verifier's two walks are the two passes of one traversal that discharges a node only where the table lists a row there, each in the context it reaches the node in, and keys each record to its rows where it is made; the anchors that keyed records to rows afterwards, the fallback that discharged a row no record reached and the walk's own site decisions are gone. The verdicts are unchanged over the 323 corpus programs, record for record, with two moves outside it:
  - An operation or a call inside an `assume` condition is no obligation: an `assume` is not compiled, so a narrowing, a trapping operation or a call's precondition in its condition is never evaluated. `vera verify` reported an `E503` for such a narrowing, an `E501` for such a call, and an `E532` warning for such a call whose precondition it cannot state, each with no record beside it; it now reports none of them, and the `assume` keeps its `W003`. *Upgrade:* a program whose only error was a narrowing or a call precondition inside an `assume` now verifies.
  - The `INT_MIN / -1` overflow of a signed quotient written inside a refinement predicate is discharged at the declaration, with the predicate's other operations: `{ @Int | 100 / @Int.0 > 0 }` reads its overflow Tier 1 where it read Tier 3.
