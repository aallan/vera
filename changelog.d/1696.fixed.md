- **A goal over a `Float64` stand-in is undecided without asking Z3** ([#1696](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1696)).  A value the verifier cannot translate at `Float64`, such as `int_to_float(@Int.0)`, is a stand-in carrying no fact a refutation may rest on, and a goal over it was asked as a floating-point query that took 1 to 4 s to return the countermodel the rule then set aside; it is Tier 3 without the query, as a symbolic floating-point argument of `float_to_int` already was.  `vera verify examples/maximum_syntax.vera` takes 1.6 s where it took 9.1 s, its two such goals at the same tier.  A call's result over such a stand-in still carries its callee's `ensures` and is asked, so `examples/ephemeris.vera`'s `right_ascension`, whose range comes back through `wrap_deg`, still proves; a goal true for every float value over the stand-in itself no longer proves, and no corpus program has one.
