The pre-scans, `_scan_io_ops` and `_scan_expr_for_handlers`, are the fold's traversal, which meets a handler's body before its clauses where the hand walks met the clauses first: a handler whose clause and body each hold the first handler of a `State` or `Exn` family declares the body's family first, E607 and E612 name the body's cell or payload when the clause holds one too, and an E618 on a closure in each is reported for the body's first, the order E617 already had.
