- **`vera test --distrust` reads a trap through the reconciliation's relation, so a trap at a check whose record stands at another node is attributed** ([#1633](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1633)).  The tester joined the check a trap fired at to the verifier's record by exact span, so a refined parameter's guard (at the parameter's type, its record at the caller's argument), a destructure's or a pattern's binder guard (its record at the value it takes apart), a refined return's guard (its record at the body), an arm's guard (its record at the join) and a callee's `requires` check (its record at the call) joined nothing, and a false proof behind one read `unattributed`.  `_TrapIndex` reads `vera.reconcile.join`'s relation, every record with every check standing for it whatever its status (`Reconciliation.answers`), and the checks a function makes on entry (`Reconciliation.entry`), each of which stands, in a trial, for the calls written in the functions the trial's run reaches (`Reconciliation.entry_calls`): another caller's record is neither its proof nor its guard, and a reached call with no record, one whose precondition the verifier discharged, keeps a trap there unattributed; the exact-span reading, the return type's special case and the hand-written prologue test are gone.  Over the 1,561 non-prelude checks the distrust corpus compiles at c9235415, the relation joins 1,364 to a record, against 1,328 by span; the 197 it joins to none are 92 checks with no record at all (W004), 73 in imported bodies this run did not verify, and 32 `requires` checks whose calls' discharged preconditions the verifier does not record.
