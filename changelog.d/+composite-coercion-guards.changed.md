- **`safe_divide`'s `requires` is `@Int.1 > 0`, so its division proves whole.**  `@Int.0 / @Int.1` traps on two divisors, zero and `-1` under `INT_MIN`, and the verifier obligates the first (`E526`) but not yet the second ([#1598](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1598)), so `requires(@Int.1 != 0)` left one input to a trap while the landing page says the clause makes the division proved.  A positive divisor excludes both.  `examples/safe_divide.vera`, README.md, SKILL.md, EXAMPLES.md, FAQ.md, PYPI_README.md, spec chapters 5 and 6 and the landing page carry the new clause, and every documented call keeps a positive divisor; spec chapter 5's two-`requires` variant now verifies and runs to 5, where its `ensures(@Int.result >= 0)` failed under a negative divisor.  The examples whose point is the zero-divisor obligation itself (FAQ.md's answer on primitive operations, SKILL.md's `fn divide`, spec chapter 6's `apply_div` and the `ch06_requires` and `ch04_primitive_obligations` conformance programs) keep `@Int.1 != 0`.
