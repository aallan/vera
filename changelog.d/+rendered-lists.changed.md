- **The effect names and the limitation tables are rendered from one source each** ([#1653](https://github.com/aallan/vera/pull/1653)).  `python scripts/render_lists.py --write` writes them between `render_lists` markers: the built-in effect names, from the registry `vera effects --json` publishes, into the vscode, TextMate and Vim grammars and the two extension READMEs; and the limitation tables, from `limitations.json`, a new file beside `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` with one record per limitation (the issues it cites, and the cells each table that carries it shows), into `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, `vera/README.md`, spec chapters 8, 9 and 12, `SKILL.md` and `LSP_SERVER.md`.  Every table renders byte for byte as it was written; the five effect lists take the registry's order, with `HttpServer` before `Http` in the two regex alternations.  `render_lists.py --check` names each block that is not what `--write` writes, and `check_doc_counts.py --release` runs it, so a release cannot ship a stale block.  `check_editor_grammars.py`, its hook and its CI step are deleted.  `check_limitations_sync.py` loses its copy check: its hook and lint step check that `limitations.json` loads and that the two hand-kept Bugs headings survive, and its weekly `--check-states` reads the issues from `limitations.json` and from the Issue columns of those two Bugs tables.  The site assets drop the `render_lists` markers with the documentation markers, so `docs/` is unchanged.
