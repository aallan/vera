- **`&&`, `||` and `==>` do not evaluate a right operand the left one decides, in compiled code and in the verifier** ([#1501](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1501)).  Spec §4.6 says the right operand of `&&` and `||` is not evaluated if the left operand determines the result, but code generation lowered the three operators to an instruction over two evaluated operands (`i32.and`, `i32.or`, and `i32.eqz` then `i32.or` for `==>`), so a right operand that traps or performs an effect ran after the left one had decided: `@Int.0 != 0 && 10 / @Int.0 > 0` trapped on `0`, `@Int.0 >= 0 && @Int.0 < 3 && @Array<Int>.0[@Int.0] > 15` trapped at the index on `5` and on `-1`, and `true || say(())` printed.  The verifier matched that lowering and took no left operand as a premise of its right one, so the same division was refused `E526` and the index recorded Tier 3.  The one lowering every compiled position reaches (a body, a compiled `requires` or `ensures`, a refinement guard, a quantifier predicate) now compiles `a && b` to `if a then b else 0`, `a || b` to `if a then 1 else b` and `a ==> b` to `if a then b else 1`, and the SMT translation and the walks that obligate an expression (primitive operations, binding-site `@Nat` narrowings, the `decreases` measure's recursive calls) take the left operand as the premise of the right one, its negation for `||`, as an `if` arm takes its condition: an obligation in the right operand is discharged under it, and a fact learned there (a callee's assumed `ensures`, a refined binder's predicate) is assumed under it.  A left operand the verifier cannot translate is stood for by a fresh Boolean, so a fact learned in the right operand stays conditional on a value nothing states.  Spec §5.2.2's equivalence of several `requires` clauses with one joined by `&&` now holds: `requires(@Int.0 >= 1 && need_pos(@Int.0) > 0)` verifies as `requires(@Int.0 >= 1) requires(need_pos(@Int.0) > 0)` does, where it was refused `E501`, and on `0` both fail the function's own precondition without calling `need_pos`.  The first decision of PR [#1486](https://github.com/aallan/vera/pull/1486), that `&&` and `||` are not premises while both operands are evaluated, is reversed with it: `{ @Nat | @Nat.0 == 3 || @Nat.0 - string_length("abcde") > 0 }` accepts `3` where its guard trapped, and a postcondition that rested on a refined binder bound in `&&`'s right operand, which now does not run where the left operand is false, is left to its runtime check (`E522`) where it was proved.  Spec §11.3.3's table now gives the three lowerings, and §2.6, §5.2.2 and §6.4.3 state the premise.  `tests/test_short_circuit_1501.py` holds the class: 105 cells over the position (a body, a `requires`, an `ensures` over `@result`, a refinement predicate at a boundary, a `forall` predicate, a `where` helper, a closure, an `assert`), the operator, and what the right operand holds (a division, an index, a call whose `requires` the left operand establishes, an `@Int` narrowed into a `@Nat` parameter, and an `IO.print` in the three effectful positions), each compiled, run and verified beside the `if` §4.6 defines it as and held to the same records and runs, all 105 red at fa958730; and 24 cells for the issue's programs, the premise ending with the right operand, an operation in the left operand, an untranslatable left operand, the premise's own translation and each walk.  `tests/conformance/ch04_short_circuit.vera` runs the guards, the effects and the joined `requires`.
