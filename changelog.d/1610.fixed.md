- **Breaking: `vera/proposeEdit`, `vera/strengthenContract` and `vera/addEffect` refuse an edit that introduces an obligation the solver did not finish (`timeout`), or moves one to `timeout` from `tier3`, `tier3_unguarded` or `violated`** ([#1610](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1610)).  The gate refused an edit that introduced a `tier3` obligation and applied one that introduced a `timeout`: the proof delta files a `timeout` under `timed_out`, which no conjunct of the gate read.  A check the solver did not finish is not a proof, so the gate now asks one rule of every obligation the edit leaves: it is `verified`, or it is what it was.  An obligation that timed out before the edit and times out after it, moved or not, still applies.  Upgrade: a client that proposed such an edit and relied on its being applied passes `"force": true` to `vera/proposeEdit`, which says out loud that the edit leaves a check unproved; `vera/strengthenContract` and `vera/addEffect` take no `force`, and their responses do not return the edited source (`applied`, `ok`, `proof_delta`, `diagnostics`, `client`, `client_reason`, and `rewritten` for `vera/addEffect`), so the client builds the full edited text itself and sends it to `vera/proposeEdit` with `"force": true`.
