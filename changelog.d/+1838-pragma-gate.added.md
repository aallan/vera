- **A mutation pragma is filed under the line it is written on** ([#1838](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1838)).  mutmut files a `# pragma: no mutate` by its place in the syntax tree, so one written after the closing bracket of a statement that spans lines protected the statement's first line instead of its own.  `scripts/check_pragma_placement.py` reads each pragma as the engine does, with `tokenize` and without the engine, and refuses one filed under another line, a bare one alone on its line and one no visitor reads; it runs in the commit hook and CI's `lint` job, and its tests hold it to mutmut's own visitor in the Mutation (diff) lane.  The nine pragmas it found under `vera/` are moved onto the lines they answer or deleted, and the mutants the old places suppressed by accident are answered.  Four pragmas whose reason was "unreachable" also hid the mutants that make their condition true, which the tests kill: their conditions are split into one test per operand, and no pragma is left on them.
