- **One rule for an expression the verifier cannot translate** ([#1696](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1696), the lines of [#1470](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1470), [#1571](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1571), [#1595](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1595) and [#1601](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1601) among them).  An expression outside the decidable fragment — a closure applied through `apply_fn`, an effect operation's result, a generic call, a fold, a `Map` or `Set` built in place, a `match` on `map_get`'s result, a refinement predicate calling a host function — is one stand-in of its checked type's sort, named by the expression and read as one term by every pass, carrying its type's facts: `Nat`'s `>= 0`, and a refinement's translatable conjuncts over any base (`hd(@NEI.0)` under `type NEI = { @Array<Int> | array_length(@Array<Int>.0) > 0 }` proves against a callee's `requires` of the same predicate).  The context translates around it: a branch's facts hold under the condition's stand-in, a `match` arm runs under the earlier arms' negations and its binders read the payload's declared type, a call with a stand-in argument keeps its `ensures`, a literal keeps its length, and a `State` handler whose clauses each resume once has its body's value.  A refutation that rests on a stand-in reports the obligation undecided, for every kind, unless the goal is false for every value the stand-in could take: one whose goal names a stand-in, that runs under a condition naming one, or that needs one at a single value.  A refutation tied to a stand-in only through an `assert` over a range of the goal's own inputs still stands.  A callee's `ensures` is no longer assumed on runs that never make the call (#1571's false Tier 1 is a runtime check), an `assume` over a `let`-bound call reaches the `ensures` (#1601), a recursive call with an untranslatable argument keeps its `decreases` and precondition proofs (#1595), and a refutation no longer rests on a value the type forbids (#1470).
