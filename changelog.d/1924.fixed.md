- A destructuring `let` of a user data type is translated ([#1924](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1924)): the linker now spells its constructor by its link name (`Box$MkBox`), as it spells every other constructor, so `let MkBox<@Int> = MkBox(5);` under `ensures(@Int.result == 5)` is proved where the block had no term and the postcondition was a runtime check (`E522`); a shape named by its type (`let Box<…>`), an alias of it or an imported type, reads the value's one constructor.
