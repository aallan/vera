- **The nightly mutation sweep decides mutants again after it drops a test from the oracle** ([#1822](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1822)).  `tests/test_composite_coercion_guards.py` parametrised one test over a generator, which has its cells in the first collection of a process alone; mutmut runs its stats pass, its listing and its clean run as sessions of one process, so on a night's rerun the clean run asked for 172 cells its collection no longer had, pytest answered with its usage-error exit, and three nights decided no mutant.  The instrument parametrises over a list; pytest's `PytestRemovedIn10Warning`, which names every such usage, is an error in the suite's configuration, so the pull-request gate refuses the next one; the sweep's own tests collect the oracle twice in one interpreter and ask for every recorded id the second time; and the sweep names an exception that ends the engine's log in its record, where this stop was recorded with no name.  Beside it, two nights' finds take their fixes: the generator models an `if` whose branch holds a `let` the verifier does not translate as the unconstrained value the verifier sees ([#1696](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1696)'s member of 2026-11-02, generator seed 2900205), and the distrust corpus's mode differential compares what `--distrust` decides about a function it does not distrust rather than the trials Z3 drew, which two runs of one process can draw apart ([#1785](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1785)).
