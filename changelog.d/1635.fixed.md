- `examples/factorial.vera` states its domain, `requires(@Nat.0 <= 20)` ([#1635](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1635)): its contract admitted every `@Nat` and the run trapped with `Integer overflow` from 21, so `vera test --distrust` failed 80 of 100 trials.  Every `factorial` the documents teach with `ensures(@Nat.result >= 1)` takes the same precondition.
