- **`vera/lowering.py` holds what code generation lowers, one rule per construct, and E340 refuses at check what it does not lower** ([#1597](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1597), [#1512](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1512), [#1499](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1499)).  The rules are the effect a handler handles, a `State` cell and an `Exn` payload, a `State` clause's resume, a quantifier's predicate and bound, the abilities lowered, and E339's shadowing judgement with its re-entry cap.  The checker applies each where its construct is written, and code generation asks the same function at its own refusal site, where a refusal is an internal error (E699) that names the rule.  E340, "Code generation cannot lower this construct", is new; E179, E128 and E339 keep their rules.
