- The nightly mutation sweep keeps each mutant as the engine decides it ([#1838](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1838)): a window partly decided keeps its decided mutants and becomes a decided window with its last, and the next night hands mutmut the undecided mutants by name and no others. mutmut runs a bin's mutants in ascending order of estimated cost across the whole bin, so under the rule that counted a window only when it was whole the nights of 7 to 10 November 2026 decided at least 201 mutants and recorded 34, each night deciding the same cheap mutants again. The state is schema 3: a window's `partial` decisions, a `slow` column, and each survivor a record of its line, the hash of that line's text and the test files it ran; a schema 2 state loads with no window partly decided and its cycle goes on. A slice's record carries each mutant's duration and mutmut's estimate, the engine's phase times, its last count and the runner's memory floor and swap peak.
- A budget stop is a normal stop for the nightly mutation sweep: the rule "its budget ran out before its first mutant" fired on nine slice-nights whose engines had decided 9 to 39 mutants each, and is gone. A night is red for a slice whose engine or lane failed, more than two slices with no record, records that disagree, an empty plan while the cycle is open, or a second night in a row that decides no mutant; a cycle closes when its sample is decided, whatever else the night did.
- The nightly mutation sweep plans the night over every job: the functions with undecided mutants, in the tree's order up to the night's capacity, go the dearest first into the job holding least, where on four nights three of seven jobs planned nothing while 362 mutants waited; the estimate learns from each decided mutant's recorded duration, its clamp widened from 5.0 to 100.
- Each slice of the nightly mutation sweep runs two mutmut workers, and `tests/mutation_oracle.py` ends a mutant's run at 2,400 s with a timer in mutmut's worker, naming it in a file the slice reads: the mutant is `slow`, decided for the cycle, counted apart, never caught and never a survivor. With four workers a bin's survivors ran together at its end and took the runner to 269 to 469 MiB available with up to 3 GiB of swap, and two bins lost their runners twice.
- Both mutation lanes' clean run is the covering tests of the functions they mutate, not the whole selection: mutmut reads the clean run's tests off the names it is handed, and a glob such as `key__mutmut_*`, which it matches against the functions' names, named none, so each sweep slice ran its whole oracle clean, 40 to 80 minutes on one core. The sweep hands mutmut each mutant by name, and the diff lane each changed function's first mutant by name beside its glob.
