- **The SMT layer's translation and two of the verifier's walks are on `vera/fold.py`.**  `SmtContext.translate_expr()` dispatches through a `fold.dispatch` table, so a new `Expr` class fails the import of `vera.smt` until it has an arm.  `fold.contexts()` is a context walk: it yields every node with the context a consumer's step gives it, asks the step for each child's context only once the previous child's subtree has been visited, and refuses a step that leaves a child out.  The verifier's `decreases` walk and its §6.4.3 primitive-operation walk are context walks, each class's arm a step that runs its work where the recursive walk ran it, so a class with children that no arm names is walked rather than passed over.  The walker-coverage gate guards three walkers: the verifier's `@Nat`-binding walk and code generation's two compilability scans.  Verdicts, obligations and WAT are identical over the corpus.
