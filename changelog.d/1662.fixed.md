- **`scripts/check_test_drivers.py` sees a stage function or a CLI command that a test helper reaches through a value, not only one it calls by its name** ([#1662](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1662)).  The call's target is followed through the names it is assigned, the loops that bind it, the containers it is read out of (a dict's values, a list's, tuple's or set's elements), the operands of a conditional or an `or`, a parameter's default, `getattr` on a module holding stage entry points, and the arguments the helper's callers in the same module hand it.  The four helpers that drove the CLI that way, the `_cli` of `tests/test_self_qualified_calls_1558.py`, `tests/test_prelude_named_imports_1559.py` and `tests/test_warning_severity_1513.py` and the `_run_cmd` of `tests/test_nested_ctor_sort_1360.py`, run their commands through `tests/pipeline.py`'s `cli()`.  The one other helper the gate now sees, the spy in `tests/test_tester_artifacts.py` that calls the real `vera.codegen.compile` to keep the WAT `vera test` compiles, is on its allowlist with that reason, which goes from 21 entries to 22.
