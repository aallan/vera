- The nightly mutation sweep draws its sample by line rather than by mutant ([#1829](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1829)): a line of a function is in a cycle's sample by a hash of the cycle, the function and the line, with every mutant on it, and a function's sampled lines reach mutmut as the diff lane's window does, so the copy holds the sample's mutants alone. A sample of mutants drawn one by one filled a job with bins of 150 to 200 functions, since mutmut generates a kept function's mutants whole, and those bins spent 30 minutes to four hours generating and compiling the copy before the first test ran. The sweep's state is schema 2, each function's mutants by line and its sampled window's verdicts, a survivor named with its line; a schema 1 state loads with its counts and its nights and starts its cycle afresh; and the 21:00 UTC schedule runs again.
