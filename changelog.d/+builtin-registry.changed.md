- **A built-in function is one record in the built-in registry, `vera/builtins.py`, which the checker, code generation and `vera builtins` read** ([#735](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/735)).  Each of the 164 is a `BuiltinSpec`: its signature, which `TypeEnv` registers as it did; its `since`; its lowering, by an emitter of `WasmContext` written in place (88), through a host import (53) or as a prelude body (23); and the domain of `string_char_code`, from which `vera/builtin_domains.py` now derives its table.  `_translate_call` lowers a built-in through one lookup of its record, in the argument convention its emitter takes, where it tested the call against 135 branches.  `TypeEnv`'s 164 registrations, the eight host-import family sets of `vera/codegen/compilability.py` and each built-in's version, which `vera/_since.py` held, are read from the records.  Every `(import "vera" ...)` line a module declares is rendered from the registry's 83 rows over 18 families, the `Map`, `Set` and `Decimal` ones included, whose signatures their emitters wrote, so a host import's WAT signature is written once.  Compiled WAT, `vera check --json` and `vera verify --json` are identical to the base's over the examples, the conformance programs, the probes, the documentation's blocks and a call to every built-in, and `vera builtins --json` is byte-identical.  `tests/test_builtin_registry.py` holds every record to the checker's arity, its emitter, the imports a call to it declares, and the hosts that bind them.
