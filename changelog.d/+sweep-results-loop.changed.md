- The nightly mutation sweep's results go to one standing issue, `Mutation sweep results (standing)` (labelled `testing` and `mutation-sweep`), which the workflow finds by its title and creates when it is absent, and no night closes. Every scheduled night posts one comment there, red or green: the commit and the run, the mutants decided that night by verdict, the cycle's progress, the slices, and the night's new survivors counted by module, in at most 25 lines (three when nothing was decided). A cycle's close posts its score by module beside the cycle before's, and its survivors as a task list grouped by module, each line the file, the line and the mutation mutmut made (the engine's new `show-mutations` command, run with `uv.lock`'s engine on the night that renders the comment), with the rule a survivor ends by: a killing cell, `# pragma: no mutate` with its reason, or a deletion. The stitch job renders the comments (`scripts/mutate_sweep.py comment`, from the `night-report.json` the stitch now writes beside the state), and a new `results` job with `issues: write` alone posts them; each comment's first line is a marker, so a rerun posts nothing twice. A cycle's close keeps its record in the state (`comments`, the last three closed cycles, newest first, each named by its number and its first night): its counts by module, each listed mutant with its module, function, number, line and status, and the engine's version, so a cycle whose comment was not rendered is rendered from it on a later night, even after the next cycle closes. The comment is rendered once and its parts (at most 10) kept beside the record, so every later night posts the same parts, each whose marker the issue lacks; a render past those bounds is neither kept nor posted. The engine shows the mutations only when its version is the one that ran the cycle, and its calls share 300 s of the render step's 600 s. A module's tasks, or the slow and at-import lists, longer than a comment are cut between two entries, the heading repeated. A night whose stitch rendered no comment, because it failed or never ran, posts two marked lines linking the run, and comments that exist but fail to download fail the `results` job so it can be rerun. A red dispatched night's comment points at the stitch summary alone, since the tracking issue takes scheduled nights. A dispatched night posts only with the new `post` input, and says so in its first line, naming the ref it ran on. Only a run on `main` whose stitch uploaded its state posts a cycle's close comment; a dispatched run on another ref renders it into the artifact, says in its stitch summary that it was not posted, and its night's comment points at the artifact. A cycle's marker carries the date of its first night beside its number (`cycle=N from=YYYY-MM-DD gen=G part=K`, `G` the id of the run that created the state, absent from an older state), so a reset state cannot collide with an older cycle. The red path's tracking issue is now found by its own title, so a failure is never reported on the standing issue. A closed cycle's record keeps the cycle before it per module (`previous`), for the comparison.
