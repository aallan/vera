- The nightly mutation sweep's results go to one standing issue, `Mutation sweep results (standing)` (labelled `testing` and `mutation-sweep`), which the workflow finds by its title and creates when it is absent, and no night closes. Every scheduled night posts one comment there, red or green: the commit and the run, the mutants decided that night by verdict, the cycle's progress, the slices, and the night's new survivors counted by module, in at most 25 lines (three when nothing was decided). A cycle's close posts its score by module beside the cycle before's, and its survivors as a task list grouped by module, each line the file, the line and the mutation mutmut made (the engine's new `show` command, run with `uv.lock`'s engine on a closing night), with the rule a survivor ends by: a killing cell, `# pragma: no mutate` with its reason, or a deletion. The stitch job renders the comments (`scripts/mutate_sweep.py comment`, from the `night-report.json` the stitch now writes beside the state), and a new `results` job with `issues: write` alone posts them; each comment's first line is a marker, so a rerun posts nothing twice, and the last closed cycle's comment is posted once when the issue has none for it. A dispatched night posts only with the new `post` input, and says so in its first line. The red path's tracking issue is now found by its own title, so a failure is never reported on the standing issue. A closed cycle's record keeps the cycle before it per module (`previous`), for the comparison.
