- The nightly mutation sweep's results go to one standing issue, `Mutation sweep results (standing)` (labelled `testing` and `mutation-sweep`), which the workflow finds by its title and creates when it is absent, and no night closes. Every scheduled night posts one comment there, red or green: the commit and the run, the mutants decided that night by verdict, the cycle's progress, the slices, and the night's new survivors counted by module, in at most 25 lines (three when nothing was decided). A cycle's close posts its score by module beside the cycle before's, and its survivors as a task list grouped by module, each line the file, the line and the mutation mutmut made (the engine's new `show-mutations` command, run with `uv.lock`'s engine on the night that renders the comment), with the rule a survivor ends by: a killing cell, `# pragma: no mutate` with its reason, or a deletion. The stitch job renders the comments (`scripts/mutate_sweep.py comment`, from the `night-report.json` the stitch now writes beside the state), and a new `results` job with `issues: write` alone posts them; each comment's first line is a marker, so a rerun posts nothing twice. A cycle's close comment is rendered once and its parts kept in the state (`comments`, the last three closed cycles, newest first, at most 10 parts each), so every later night posts the same parts, each whose marker the issue lacks, even after the next cycle closes; a render past those bounds is neither kept nor posted. A module's tasks, or the slow and at-import lists, longer than a comment are cut between two entries, the heading repeated. A night whose stitch rendered no comment, because it failed or never ran, posts two marked lines linking the run, and comments that exist but fail to download fail the `results` job so it can be rerun. A red dispatched night's comment points at the stitch summary alone, since the tracking issue takes scheduled nights. A dispatched night posts only with the new `post` input, and says so in its first line, naming the ref it ran on. Only a run on `main` posts a cycle's close comment; a dispatched run on another ref renders it into the artifact and says in its stitch summary that it was not posted. A cycle's marker carries the date of its first night beside its number (`cycle=N from=YYYY-MM-DD part=K`), so a reset state cannot collide with an older cycle. The red path's tracking issue is now found by its own title, so a failure is never reported on the standing issue. A closed cycle's record keeps the cycle before it per module (`previous`), for the comparison.
