- **Breaking: a narrowing is no longer proved from the fact its own reader reads** ([#1897](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1897)).  A `match` arm that binds a component takes the component's declared refinement as a fact, read off the scrutinee's declared type.  Where that type was given by a narrowing in the same function (`let @Tuple<Small, Int> = @Tuple<Int, Int>.0`, a constructor field, a tuple component, a store into an array, a `Map` or a `Set`, a `put`, an argument, a return), the fact is the narrowing's own goal, and it was asserted before the narrowing was asked: the narrowing was recorded `verified`, and so was every obligation over the same value, while an `Option` or data payload reached its reader with every gate green.  A producer's own obligation is now discharged without the fact it publishes (spec §6.2.5, §2.6.4): the facts a reader takes off a type a narrowing in the function gave the value are withheld, as a disclosed producer's are, and the narrowing's own obligation is offered them on no attempt.  What moves, for a program that narrows a composite below its head and reads it: the narrowing is refuted (`E505`) where nothing else proves it, or disclosed (`E506`) at a payload no check covers, or, at a tuple component refined outside the solver at a `let` or a `put`, it is the `E541` the same `let` draws with no reader (#1542); an `ensures`, a call's precondition or another narrowing of the same value that held only from the reader's fact is Tier 3 (`E534`, `E532`) with its check, where it was `verified`.  No run changes.  A narrowing the program proves (from a `requires`, a branch condition or the value's own type) proves as before, and so does every obligation its reader proves, since the premises that prove the narrowing are in the reader's context.
