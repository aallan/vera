- **The `@Nat` → `@Int` widening check, the range check on a `@Nat` termination measure, and the typing of a literal by its value** ([#1504](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1504)).  With `Nat` the non-negative i64 none of them has anything to check.  Gone: the `nat_to_int_coerce` obligation and check kind, its diagnostics **E530** (a widening provably above i64.MAX) and **E531** (a widening neither proved nor guarded), the `widen_guard` trap kind and every widening guard spec §11.2.1 listed; the `decreases_bound` obligation and its diagnostics **E536** and **E537**, which held a `@Nat` measure to the i64 range the guard compares in; the u64 range of the overflow obligations; the sign-provenance classifiers of `vera/narrowing.py`; and spec §4.2's rule 3 (a type argument fixed by its literals' values) and rule 2's exception for a negative literal beside a non-negative one.  The change takes 4,413 lines out of `vera/` and adds 1,157, most of them `vera/verifier.py`'s (1,367), `vera/narrowing.py`'s (930) and `vera/wasm/operators.py`'s (691).
