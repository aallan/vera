- **The test suite drives the compiler through one driver, `tests/pipeline.py`.**  Its `parse`, `check`, `verify`, `compile`, `run` and `contract_test` each run their stage and the stages before it in process, wired as the CLI wires them, and return the stage's diagnostics, its artefact and the envelope `vera <stage> --json` prints; reading an artefact a stage did not make raises what stopped it, and `cli()` runs `vera.cli.main` in process.  `tests/test_pipeline.py` holds every stage to its command over the whole corpus.
  - `tests/checker_helpers.py`, `tests/verifier_helpers.py`, `tests/codegen_helpers.py` and `tests/module_fixture_helpers.py` are layers over the driver.  The 71 members of the 21 groups of local drivers with identical bodies, and 13 drivers in the same 35 test files that those call or that copy them with small variations, import a shared helper or call the driver instead, and the outcome of every one of the 26,740 tests compared is unchanged.
  - `scripts/check_test_drivers.py` (pre-commit and CI `lint`) fails a test helper that drives a stage itself rather than through `tests/pipeline.py`.  Its allowlist names the 242 helpers that still do, each with its reason, and an entry that names no driver fails the gate, so the list only shrinks.
  - Four tests that pass without measuring what they claim are filed as [#1654](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1654) and [#1655](https://github.com/aallan/vera/issues/1655).
